EZ-Fold Chair

The EZ-Fold project developed a space-efficient, ergonomic chair to assist elderly individuals in putting on shoes, addressing limited space and stability needs in Korean apartments. The design features foldable elements, sturdy materials, and mechanisms to aid in sitting and standing safely.

Design & Fabrication Process

Research & Design Opportunity

The starting problem: elderly people in Korea struggle to put on shoes, thanks to limited mobility and doorways barely wide enough to stand in. Research included:

  • Looking at existing shoe-assist products and where they fall short on space and ergonomic support.
  • Market research on Korea's aging population and the growing demand for products that support independent living.
  • Client interviews on balance, leg strength, hip movement, and how hard it actually is to reach a shoe from standing.
  • Population and anthropometric data, to size the chair correctly and keep it safe to use.

Exploration of Ideas

Early brainstorming skipped constraints on purpose, to get a wide spread of ideas on the table — different folding mechanisms, support structures, overall shapes. Sketches came first, before anything got narrowed down.

Development of Ideas

Four of the strongest ideas went into full development: 3D CAD models, scaled physical models, and FEA on the highest-stress points of each. Every concept came with its own tradeoffs, weighed directly against the others before picking a final direction.

Concept Designs & Analysis:

Orthographic Assembly Drawing

The final design pulls the strongest elements from every earlier concept. Materials and components were picked one at a time, each for a specific reason — not just what looked good on paper.

Key Components:

  • Seat Cushioning: Faux leather and polyurethane foam for comfort and durability.
  • Seat Body & Chair Base: CNC milled 40mm Maple Wood Board, chosen for its strength, durability, and ease of cleaning.
  • Linear Bearing: Ensures smooth up/down movement of the chair.
  • Stainless Steel Pole & Peg End: Provides high tensile strength and stability.
  • Back Bracket: Laser-cut stainless steel for secure wall mounting.
  • Gas Strut: Integrated to assist with opening/closing and to absorb impact when sitting, enhancing user safety and convenience.
  • Joining Components: Screws, nuts & bolts, and stainless steel U-brackets for strong, disassemblable connections.

Manufacturing Plan

The manufacturing plan lays out equipment, scheduling, quality checks, and risk points for every stage of the build. Key steps:

  • Sawing and sanding of maple wood components (seat body, chair base, peg ends).
  • Attaching the leather seat cushion and linear bearings.
  • Precision laser cutting of metal sheets for the back bracket.
  • Assembly of the seat body and chair base with stainless steel poles and peg ends.
  • Attachment and calibration of the gas strut.
  • Final installation and secure mounting of the chair onto a wall.

Quality checks ran at every stage — measurements, connections, overall stability. This is a chair someone's grandparent stands on to get dressed. It has to hold.
