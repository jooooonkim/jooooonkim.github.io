About Me

I'm a mechanical engineering student at The Cooper Union, focused on mechanical design, autonomous systems, and controls. I've built drone swarm control systems and raced autonomous vehicles in competition — work that's put me on both sides of CAD models and code. I grew up moving between countries and speaking multiple languages, which turns out to matter more on a team than people expect. Mostly I like taking a problem apart until I understand why it's hard, then building something that actually works.
